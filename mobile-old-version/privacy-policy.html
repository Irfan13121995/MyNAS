Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Data Collection and Usage

Personal NAS is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any third-party servers. All your files, photos, videos, and data are securely transmitted directly between your mobile device and your own self-hosted personal NAS server.

Permissions Explained

To provide full functionality, the Personal NAS app requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: Used exclusively for scanning QR codes to quickly pair the app with your NAS server.
  • Media Library (Photos/Videos): Required to enable automatic backup and synchronization of your media to your NAS, and to allow you to download media from the NAS to your device.
  • Biometrics / Fingerprint: Used to secure the app and provide an app lock feature, ensuring only you can access your NAS files on your device.
  • Network & Internet: Necessary for the app to communicate with your self-hosted NAS server over local networks or the internet.
